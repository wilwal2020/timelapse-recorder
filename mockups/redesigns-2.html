Timelapse Recorder — redesign test 2

Two options. 1 is the current design with the parts you picked. 2 is Studio (B) revised, with every state of the Record tab. As before, only the look changes; everything works the same.

1 · Current, with your picks

Library and Settings follow B's layout: a dark header box with the folder size, one card per recording, and uppercase section labels. The coloured line beside recordings is gone, and Refresh sits at the top. Preview is a small button inside the file name bar, like in C; pressing it opens the preview underneath. The pill toggles have a new look: off is a plain outline with an empty ring, on is a solid blue pill with the ring filled in. You can see them in Auto record and the export options, with two other pill styles to compare below.

2 · Studio (B), revised

The round green button is gone. The big box at the top shows the state, and a wide button along its bottom does the recording: Start recording (red dot) → Recording (green, click to pause) → Paused (grey, click to resume). The box's outline and status line change colour with it: green when recording, yellow when paused, plain when not started. The Record tab's dot is green only while this document records. In the Library, the green line beside recordings is gone and Refresh is at the top.
