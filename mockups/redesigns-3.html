2 · Studio (B), round button back

The round button is back next to the timer, redrawn with classic recorder symbols. Not started and paused show a red record dot in a grey ring (click to record or resume). While recording, the button fills with colour and shows the pause symbol (click to pause). The recording state is shown twice, once in red (like a camera) and once in green; the status text, the box outline and the tab dot follow the same colour. In the Library, Open folder / Delete frames are centred, and Refresh is plain text above the recordings, the same as in option 1.
